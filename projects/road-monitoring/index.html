Overview

Designed to bridge public awareness and local government infrastructure management, the LGU Road & Transportation Monitoring System is a centralized web-based platform that brings road condition monitoring, transportation incident reporting, infrastructure project tracking, and flood susceptibility information into one accessible environment. It aims to make local road information easier to understand while providing government personnel with a more organized and efficient approach to infrastructure monitoring and management.

At its core is an interactive Live Road Monitoring Map with geolocation integration, allowing residents to identify their current location, explore reported road conditions, view transportation incidents, and locate potentially flood-prone areas. The platform also enables the public to submit incident reports and access relevant road and infrastructure information. Beyond public monitoring, it provides LGU personnel with dedicated tools for managing incoming reports, verifying incidents, assigning projects, coordinating responsible personnel, and tracking implementation progress through structured report management, role-based access, progress updates, scheduling, and document management.

Developed using PHP, MySQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the platform combines interactive mapping, database-driven workflows, and administrative management into a unified system. It further strengthens public transparency by making infrastructure project details accessible, including project budgets, fund allocations, implementation progress, and utilization records. By connecting citizen participation with organized government operations and accessible financial information, the platform promotes more informed communities, efficient infrastructure management, and greater accountability in the use of public resources.

Core modules

  • Interactive Road Map

    Visualizes road conditions, reported incidents, traffic situations, and flood susceptibility areas.

  • Reports Management

    Handles road and transportation reports, verification, and monitoring.

  • Infrastructure Projects

    Tracks project assignments, progress, and completion.

  • Flood & Hazard Monitoring

    Integrates geospatial information to identify flood-susceptible areas.

  • Feedback Management

    Provides a channel for public feedback.

  • Transparency Records

    Makes project updates and public records accessible.

  • Document Management

    Organizes supporting files and project documentation.

  • User Access Management

    Controls staff accounts, roles, and permissions.

  • AI-Integrated Chatbot

    Provides AI-powered assistance for inquiries, report submissions, and platform navigation.

Technical foundation

Server-rendered PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and integrated mapping and geospatial services.
